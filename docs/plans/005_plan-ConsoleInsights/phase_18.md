Intent: everybody can tell what every chart, summary card, table, section, badge and setting shows, in which unit, over which window and from which source; summary cards carry one primary figure and never an unlabelled second number.

- Caption writing rules (apply to every catalogue entry; the `advisor` samples against them at closure):
  1. `what` (≤ 160 characters, one sentence, plain words) says **what is measured**, e.g. "Transactions committed per second, summed over all databases." — never "Shows TPS".
  2. `source` names the real origin (PostgreSQL view/column or pglens computation) **verified against the check's SQL or the API handler** (the writer opens the code; a caption must not state a metric the check does not emit).
  3. `unit` and `window` are always stated (per second, bytes, percent; "last hour of the selected range", "latest sample", "since the last statistics reset").
  4. `howToRead` says what high/low means only where the product defines it (advisor rule thresholds, e.g. `conn.near_max` 80 % of `max_connections`; heuristic constants from phase 4); otherwise it explains how to read the shape ("a staircase means counters reset") and never claims a cause.
  5. Estimates say "estimated"; heuristics say which input they use (I-5).
  6. Acronyms must be in `glossary.ts` (TPS, WAL, LSN, XID, MVCC, TOAST, HOT, ASH, FK, I/O, CPU is plain; UTC allowed) — `Term` renders them with their definition.
  7. Cards: the label is a noun phrase naming the counted thing ("Exact duplicate indexes", not "Duplicates"); the unit is one of the closed list; detail sentences give each number its noun; zero is explained ("none found").
- Worked examples (the catalogue must have this quality): `overview.tps` — what "Transactions committed per second, summed over all databases (rollbacks are not included)."; source "pg_stat_database.xact_commit, rate per second"; window "selected time range"; howToRead "A flat line at zero means no committed work; gaps mean the agent was not reporting." `index.duplicate` — what "Groups of indexes on the same table with identical definitions; each group wastes the space and write cost of all but one index."; `index.redundant_prefix` — what "Indexes whose columns are the leading part of a longer index on the same table; the longer index can serve the same lookups."; `activity.connections_used` — what "Client connections open now compared with max_connections."; `locks.origin.pglens` — what "Sessions opened by pglens itself to monitor this server; they are not your application's workload."
- Unit list (`web/src/lib/units.ts`): `TileUnit = 'ms' | 's' | 'min' | 'h' | 'days' | '%' | 'bytes' | 'KiB/s' | 'MiB/s' | 'per second' | 'per minute' | 'rows' | 'rows/s' | 'connections' | 'sessions' | 'queries' | 'statements' | 'indexes' | 'tables' | 'databases' | 'findings' | 'alerts' | 'checks'`; `formatUnit` maps to the display string.
