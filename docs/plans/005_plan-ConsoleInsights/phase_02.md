# Phase 1 — Chart foundation and the Transactions-per-second fix

Intent: every time-series chart (Overview, ASH / wait analysis, replication lag) shows readable UTC time labels that never overlap, a continuous line for counters, no balloon markers, and states its range and step; the TPS chart is correct at 1 h, 6 h and 24 h; the ASH / wait analysis page shows one chart per wait group (CPU, I/O, locks, client, other) with a clear legend instead of one crowded stacked chart.
Prerequisites: 0.2 (visual harness).
Phase closure: P1; review by `advisor`.

## State and ownership contract
Read STATE.md §0–1 first. Open before edits; checkpoint at a recovery boundary; close with evidence. Missing design goes to the `advisor`.

## Local design context
Plan revision: 1.
- D1: rate/counter metrics are queried at `step >= max(60 s, 2 × sampling)`, gauges at `step >= sampling`. Evidence (live API, 2026-10-29): `pg_xact_commit_total` at 15 s → 111/240 buckets non-null; at 1 m → 59/60; a bucket is non-null when it holds at least one stored per-scrape rate (the server averages the rates stored in a bucket, `handleMetricsQuery`), `database_stats` samples every 30 s, so a 15 s step leaves roughly every other bucket empty, the line breaks, and each break matched the "reset" rule (null bucket with values on both sides) and drew a pin-shaped marker — those pins are the "balloons" the user sees.
- D2: time axis in UTC; `formatAxisTime(ms, spanMs)` → `HH:mm` when span <= 36 h, else `MMM d HH:mm`; `hideOverlap: true`; slider `labelFormatter` uses the same function; `formatTooltipTime(ms)` → `YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm:ss UTC`; `formatRangeLabel(from, to, stepSeconds)` → `Oct 29 09:41 → 10:41 UTC · step 1m`.
- D3: no `markPoint`; resets are dashed vertical `markLine`s (no label, `symbol: 'none'`, `silent: true`), at most 12 per series; above the cap none are drawn and the card subtitle says `N counter resets`; tooltip `trigger: 'axis'`, `confine: true`, compact formatter.
- R1: ECharts 6.1.0 typings accept `axisLabel.hideOverlap`, slider `labelFormatter`, `markLine`.
- I-1 guard: `chartStep.test.ts` and the live completeness check.
- Sampling seconds table (constants in `chartStep.ts`): `database_stats` 30, `wal` 15, `activity` 10, `checkpointer` 30, `io` 30, `host` 30, `statements` 60, `tables` 300, `indexes` 300; default 30.
- Metric kind: names ending `_total` or containing `_rate` → `rate`; the agent's counter metrics that do not follow that suffix are listed explicitly in `metricKind` (`pg_wal_wal_bytes`, `pg_wal_wal_records`, `pg_wal_wal_fpi`, `pg_wal_wal_buffers_full`, `pg_wal_wal_write`, `pg_wal_wal_sync`, `pg_wal_lsn_bytes` — all `KindCounter`, delta-converted at ingest, verify the list against `internal/check/wal.go` in 1.1 S1: `walCounterColumn` also marks every `_count`, `_bytes` and `_time` column, so the complete explicit list is derived there from the real column names and asserted by CSTEP-006); everything else (`*_ratio`, `pg_connections_*`, `pg_backends`, `host_*`, `*_bytes` gauges, `*_seconds`) → `gauge`; `metricKind` is the single place that decides and has an explicit table in its test.
- `chooseStep(from, to)` (locate with `tokensave_search`; `STEP_LADDER` 15 s, 30 s, 1 m, 5 m, 15 m, 1 h, 6 h, 1 d, <= 1000 points) stays as the base rule.
